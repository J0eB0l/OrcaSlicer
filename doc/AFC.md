# AFC lanes in Snapmaker Orca

Printers that run [AFC](https://github.com/ArmoredTurtle/AFC-Klipper-Add-On) on Klipper (Box Turtle, ACE, OpenAMS, Bambu AMS through BridgeBox, and any mix of them) show their lanes in the slicer, sync filaments from them and map project filaments to lanes before a print.

## What it does

- **Lanes panel on the Device tab.** A side panel next to the printer page lists every toolhead, every unit and every lane: color, material, vendor, remaining grams, Spoolman spool, which T number it answers to and whether it is loaded in a toolhead. Clicking a lane edits its T number, material, color, temperatures, weight or Spoolman spool and writes the change back to AFC. Lanes can be loaded, unloaded or ejected from the same popup.
- **Drying.** Units with a heater (ACE, ACE 2 Pro, Bambu AMS 2 Pro, AMS HT) show their temperature and humidity and a Dry button. The dry popup offers material presets, a temperature and time, and whether to turn the spools. A unit that is drying shows the target, the time left and a Stop button.
- **Filament sync.** The Sync filaments button fills one slicer slot per loaded AFC lane, with the lane's color, material and Spoolman data. Empty lanes get no slot. Each slot remembers the T number of its lane, and the G-code changes to that T number, so slots 1 to 3 synced from T0, T3 and T5 print as `T0`, `T3` and `T5` with no print popup. Syncing again keeps every object on its lane: an object on the T3 slot moves to the slot T3 gets in the new list, and one whose lane is empty now goes to a loaded lane. In mapping mode, a slot prints as the lane it is mapped to. A slot added by hand takes the lowest T number no synced slot has. The renumbering covers what AFC reads: `T` commands, the `T` of `M104` and `M109` (not one with an `A` parameter, which AFC reads as a Snapmaker extruder number), and `TOOL=`, `INITIAL_TOOL=` and `T<n>_TEMP=` of `PRINT_START` or `START_PRINT`. A filament number a start G-code writes anywhere else, such as another macro's parameter, stays the slot number. So do the U1's own commands, such as `SM_PRINT_PREEXTRUDE_FILAMENT INDEX=`: the U1 reads a file's filaments by slot, and a print sent from the AFC lane screen tells it each slot's toolhead. Each slot takes the filament preset named like the lane's spool, else the Generic one for its material, else any of your own presets for that material (printers without system filament presets, such as a custom Voron, only have those).
- **Spool presets.** A spool with a vendor (from Spoolman, say) gets a filament preset of its own. Sync first looks for one named after the spool's vendor and filament name, such as `Polymaker PolyLite PLA`, your own presets before the system ones. When there is none it makes `Polymaker PolyLite PLA @<printer>` from the preset the slot would otherwise get, with the spool's nozzle and bed temperatures, density, diameter and vendor, and the slot uses it. There is one such preset per vendor and filament name, shared by every spool of that filament. Later syncs put the spool's values back into a preset they made; it says so on the first line of its notes, and deleting that line keeps your own edits. Spools without a vendor keep the preset matching above. Flow K stays per slot, not in the preset.
- **Print mapping.** Before a print starts, a popup suggests a lane for every project filament (by material, color and remaining weight), lets each one be changed and applies the result to AFC with `SET_MAP`.
- **Flow K.** A lane whose Spoolman spool has a saved flow K (the pressure advance AFC autocal stores in the spool's `flow_k` field) shows it on the lane, in the lane editor and in the print popup. Sync filaments gives every slot the K of the spool it was synced from: that slot prints with it, with pressure advance turned on even if the filament preset has it off. Slots whose spool has no K keep the preset's value. Picking another filament preset for a slot drops its spool K, and the print popup notes a slot mapped to a spool whose K differs from the one it was sliced with. On printers running AFC autocal with `apply_stored_k`, AFC applies the loaded spool's K itself and ignores the slicer's.
- **Flushing on multi-toolhead printers.** On a printer with several toolheads (a U1, a toolchanger) and the multi-material "single extruder" option off, a slot knows which toolhead its lane feeds: Sync filaments records it from each lane's AFC extruder, and a slot that was not synced takes the toolhead of the lane AFC maps its T number to the next time the plate is sliced with AFC connected. The flush then only happens where filament actually changes in a nozzle. A change between two slots on one toolhead is a lane change, and its T command carries the flushing volume from the slot that toolhead held, as `T5 PURGE_LENGTH=42.5` (mm of filament), so AFC's poop purges that much. Switching to a toolhead that already holds its filament sends no purge. The flushing volumes table still sets the amount for each pair of colors. The prime tower, when it is on, still primes on every tool switch, and with it off a tool switch has no purge from the slicer. With the tower on and **Purge in prime tower** checked (printer settings, Multimaterial, Wipe tower, in Advanced mode), a lane change flushes that volume on the tower instead and its T command carries no `PURGE_LENGTH`; unchecked, the poop does it. The print popup can put a T number on a lane of another toolhead, so after it the file's `PURGE_LENGTH` values are recomputed for the lanes picked. With the tower flushing, the plate is sliced again for those lanes instead, so their lane changes flush on the tower too, and the print or upload goes out when that slice is done. Purging with the poop needs `poop` turned on in AFC, and with it on AFC also poops its own default length on a lane load that has no `PURGE_LENGTH`. Printers without AFC, Klipper or not, slice as before, and changing the printer profile forgets the synced toolheads and T numbers.
- **Dark mode.** The panel and the print popup follow the slicer's dark mode. The U1 device page switches to Snapmaker's own dark theme too; Snapmaker has not finished it, so its camera and control cards stay light.

## Which printers

- **Snapmaker U1** with AFC: connect the printer on the Device tab as usual.
- **Any other Klipper printer** with AFC: set the printer preset's host type to Octo/Klipper, enter the Moonraker address (and API key if Moonraker asks for one) in the printer's connection settings. The Device tab shows the printer's own web page (Fluidd or Mainsail) with the AFC panel next to it.

The panel stays hidden on printers without AFC.

## Notes

- AFC lane T numbers do not need to be continuous. A gap (for example `T16` to `T19` unused, `T20` on a lane) gets no slot, and the slot synced from `T20` still prints as `T20`.
- On printers other than the U1, the printer profile has to accept as many filaments as there are loaded lanes, with AFC handling the `T` commands in the G-code. A single-toolhead printer uses a single-extruder multi-material style profile; a multi-toolhead one sets its extruder count to its toolheads with that option off, so the flushing above applies.
- The panel page lives in `resources/web/afc/`. Opening `index.html?view=panel&mock=1` (or `view=print`) in a browser shows it with demo data modeled on a six-toolhead AFC printer, without the slicer.
